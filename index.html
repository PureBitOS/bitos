PureBitOS • minimal x64 operating system • v0.1

BitOS — bare metal rising.

A minimal x64 OS written primarily in assembly. It boots via Multiboot2 + GRUB, clears the VGA screen, prints a welcome message, then halts. This is the very beginning.

What it does today

  • Multiboot2 header (kernel/multiboot_header.asm) with magic 0xe85250d6
  • Links at 1M via kernel/linker.ld → iso/boot/kernel.bin
  • Clears VGA text buffer at 0xb8000 (80×25, white-on-black)
  • Prints "BitOS v0.1 - Bare metal rising! (x64 foundation)" in light green
  • Disables interrupts (cli) and halts (hlt loop)

Source of truth: kernel/boot.asm, kernel/multiboot_header.asm, iso/boot/grub/grub.cfg.
